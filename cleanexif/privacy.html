CleanExif Privacy Policy

CleanExif inspects, edits, and compresses photos on your device. The developer does not receive your photos. The app does not require an account or use advertising or third-party analytics.

Photos and processing history

You select photos using Apple’s system photo picker. For a photo stored in iCloud, Apple’s Photos service may retrieve the original. CleanExif saves a separate copy when you choose to save. Processing history and its copies are stored on your device. You can delete entries in Processing History.

Optional location features

If you request an address from a photo, CleanExif sends the photo’s embedded coordinates to Apple Maps. If you search for a place to add, the app sends the search terms you enter to Apple Maps. Neither request sends the photo or accesses your current location. You can enter coordinates offline.

Purchases

Apple handles CleanExif Pro purchases and restoration through StoreKit. CleanExif checks the transaction on your device to determine whether Pro is unlocked.
