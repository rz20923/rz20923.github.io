CleanExif Support

Help with photo privacy, compression, and CleanExif Pro.

Find a saved copy

Open Apple Photos → Collections → Recently Saved. If your library is sorted by capture date, a copy with an edited date appears under the date you selected.

Keep a Live Photo

In Photo Shrink, choose Keep Live. The app compresses the still image and keeps the paired video with its sound. The size shown in CleanExif includes both files.

Restore CleanExif Pro

Open the CleanExif Pro screen and choose Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Report an issue

Open a support issue on GitHub with the app version, iOS version, and steps to reproduce the issue. GitHub issues are public. Leave out personal photos, locations, and purchase details.
